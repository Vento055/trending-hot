The **DOT’s new refund rule** is arguably the most significant structural driver. As of October 2024, airlines must refund passengers for cancellations or "significant" changes. The tracker trend thrives because third-party sources (like FlightAware or the subreddit r/AlaskaAirlines) often provide more accurate recovery data than the airline’s own app. This is a trust deficit that keeps the search volume stable—users check the tracker because they don't trust the primary source.
