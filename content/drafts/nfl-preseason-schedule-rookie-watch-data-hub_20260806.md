## How to Use a Data Hub for Preseason Insights

For the average reader, the **NFL Preseason Schedule & Rookie Watch Data Hub** trend is only useful if they know how to operationalize it. Here is a practical guide.

### 1. Track "Snap Counts" Over Box Scores
Do not just look at a rookie's rushing yards. Look at the **snap percentage**. A rookie who plays 60% of offensive snaps in the third preseason game is likely to be a starter. A rookie who plays 10% is likely a cut candidate. Data hubs that offer this granularity are the most valuable.

### 2. Set Alert Thresholds
The best hubs allow you to set alerts for specific players. Set a threshold for "Rookie RB X: touches > 15." This turns the hub from a passive database into an **active monitoring tool**, which is the core value proposition of the tech.

### 3. Cross-Reference with Betting Lines
If you are a bettor, cross-reference the hub data with live betting lines. If a hub shows a rookie RB is taking first-team reps, and the betting line for his team's "team total rushing yards" has not moved, there may be a **value bet** opportunity.

### 4. Use the Schedule as a "Time-Filter"
Don't look at all data equally. Filter by game week. **Week 1 of preseason** is about veterans shaking off rust. **Week 3** is the "dress rehearsal" where starters play into the second half. A good hub will contextualize data based on the preseason game number.
